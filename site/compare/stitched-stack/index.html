RocketRez vs separate booking, POS and membership tools

Separate tools fit an attraction that sells one thing in one place, or one that just bought a tool it likes for each job. RocketRez fits attractions where a guest books online, eats at the café, shops on the way out and joins as a member, and someone needs to see all of it as one guest’s spend.

Where separate tools are a good fit

  • You sell one thing in one place.A single gate or a single departure doesn’t need a café till or a shop system joined to it.
  • You just bought a tool you like.If a system is new, paid for and working, check its contract term before you plan a move.
  • You want the specialist for each job.Separate tools let you pick each one on its own. The cost is the joins between them.

Where RocketRez fits better

  • Your guests buy at more than the gate.Hemker Park and Zoo ran an online booking system and an in-house one, and called it “chaos between our guests and our staff.”
  • You want one number you can grow.Every sale lands on one guest record, so spend per guest is there the same day.
  • You want one vendor to call.One support line, run by former attraction operators, for tickets, food, retail and members.
  • You want to keep the tools that work.Power BI, Zapier, Twilio SendGrid, Sensible Weather and an open API connect what stays.

Every guest’s spend on one record

The ticket, the giraffe feed, lunch and the photo add up on one guest, so per-cap is a number you read, not a spreadsheet you build.

Every channel in one order list

Online, gate, Viator and phone orders in one list, with totals that follow the filter. Pick a channel to try it.

RocketRez vs separate tools: questions buyers ask

What does RocketRez replace?

Typically the online booking tool, the gate POS, the café till, the shop system, the membership spreadsheet, a separate texting tool and the reporting spreadsheets. The checker on the pricing page lists each one.

Can we keep some of our current tools?

Yes. RocketRez connects to Power BI, Zapier (5,000+ apps), Twilio SendGrid and Sensible Weather, and has an open API for the rest.

Who moves our data?

RocketRez cleans, maps and migrates your member and product data, then checks it with you before launch.

How long does it take?

Typical implementation takes 8 to 12 weeks, planned back from your go-live date.
