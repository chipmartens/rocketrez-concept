RocketRez vs FareHarbor, Peek and Xola

FareHarbor, Peek Pro and Xola fit tour, activity and rental operators who mostly sell online and want a large, well-reviewed booking tool. RocketRez fits operators who also run a gate, a café, a shop or memberships, and want those sales and every travel-site booking on one inventory and one guest record.

Where FareHarbor, Peek and Xola are a good fit

  • You sell departures online and little else.If the booking is the whole sale, a booking tool may be all you need.
  • You want a vendor thousands of operators already use.FareHarbor says 20,000+ companies, with 1,100+ Capterra reviews. Peek and Xola each have hundreds.
  • You want a published rate.Xola publishes 2.39% plus $0.30 card processing with no subscription. RocketRez quotes yours.
  • Support is your first question.FareHarbor puts support in its headline. Xola states 24/7 support.
  • You want booking tools built for small operators.Peek pitches dynamic pricing, abandoned-booking recovery and tipping, and most of its reviewers are small operators.

Where RocketRez fits better

  • You sell more than the seat.The bar on board, the dock-side shop and the snack bar ring up on the same POS as the departure.
  • You sell on travel sites and can’t oversell.Viator, GetYourGuide, Google Things to Do, CityPASS and Rezdy sell from the same seat count as your website and the dock.
  • You run multi-stop trips or trains.Ticketing covers multi-stop trips with seating and passenger car editors, plus handheld scanning at boarding.
  • You book groups and charters.School trips, corporate charters and buyouts come with custom quotes and automated invoicing.

RocketRez vs FareHarbor, Peek and Xola: questions buyers ask

Is RocketRez a FareHarbor alternative?

For tour and boat operators who also run a shop, a snack bar or memberships, yes: those sales sit on the same inventory as your departures. If you only sell departures online, FareHarbor, Peek or Xola may be all you need.

Does RocketRez connect to Viator and GetYourGuide?

Yes, directly, along with Google Things to Do, CityPASS, Rezdy and Travel Curious. Every channel sells from one seat count.

Does RocketRez publish its card processing rate?

No. Xola publishes 2.39% plus $0.30. RocketRez puts your rate, through Stripe, Shift4, PayPal or Sola, in writing in your quote.

Can RocketRez handle charters and multi-leg trips?

Yes. Ticketing covers multi-stop trips with seating and passenger car editors, and group, charter and buyout bookings come with quotes and invoicing.
