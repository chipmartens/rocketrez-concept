RocketRez vs ROLLER

ROLLER fits family entertainment centres, trampoline parks and multi-venue groups that want a large vendor and a free trial. RocketRez fits independent zoos, aquariums, waterparks, tours and museums that earn at the gate, the café, the shop and the membership desk, and want guest texting and support from former operators.

Where ROLLER is a good fit

  • You run a family entertainment centre.Trampoline parks, bowling, play centres and skating are ROLLER’s home turf, and its customer list leans that way.
  • You want scale you can see.ROLLER says 3,500+ venues, 165 million guests and $5 billion processed a year. RocketRez hasn’t published a customer count.
  • You want to try it before you talk to anyone.ROLLER offers a 14-day free trial and named plans. RocketRez starts with a demo and a written quote.
  • You weigh review volume and recency.About 150 Capterra reviews, many from 2025 and 2026. RocketRez has 22, none newer than mid 2024.
  • You want round-the-clock support as standard.ROLLER states 24/7 support. RocketRez states 24/7 for critical issues in peak periods.

Where RocketRez fits better

  • You run a zoo, aquarium, waterpark, boat tour or museum.RocketRez is built for gated venues with a café, a shop and members, and its customers are those venues.
  • You want to text guests from the ticketing system.Relay sends the add-on before arrival and the rain-delay notice on the day. ROLLER bills SMS as a separate fee.
  • You want a pricing model that follows your season.Pay a fixed fee, or per ticket or a percentage of sales, with card processing in the written quote.
  • You want support from people who’ve run a gate.Reunion Tower hears back “within a couple of hours, not waiting days or weeks.”

RocketRez vs ROLLER: questions buyers ask

Is RocketRez cheaper than ROLLER?

Neither publishes prices. ROLLER lists named plans plus usage, extra-device and SMS fees. RocketRez quotes a fixed fee or a usage-based price with every fee shown, card processing included. Compare the two written quotes line by line.

Which is better for a zoo or aquarium?

ROLLER lists zoos and aquariums among its industries, and its customer list leans to family entertainment. RocketRez is built around gated venues with a café, a shop and members. Ask each vendor for a reference at a venue like yours.

Does RocketRez have a free trial?

No. RocketRez starts with a demo and a written quote. ROLLER offers a 14-day free trial.

Can RocketRez text our guests?

Yes. Relay texts guests before, during and after the visit from inside the ticketing system, with a two-way inbox and broadcasts for weather and schedule changes.

How long does switching from ROLLER take?

Typical RocketRez implementation takes 8 to 12 weeks, planned back from your go-live date, with member and product data migrated for you.
