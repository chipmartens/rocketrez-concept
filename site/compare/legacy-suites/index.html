Where legacy suites are a good fit

  • Fundraising and donors sit at the centre.Tessitura and Altru put donations, memberships and tickets in one CRM. RocketRez’s published modules don’t include donor management.
  • You run a very large park or many sites.accesso serves 1,100+ venues in 31 countries; Gateway lists admission control and resource management for big operations.
  • You want a long track record.Centaman and Blackbaud each cite 30+ years.
  • You have a specialist to run it.Tessitura and Altru reviewers say a specialist or a full-time data manager gets the most from them.

Where RocketRez fits better

  • Seasonal staff need to sell on day one.Chicago Line Cruises: “I learned the system in about ten minutes.”
  • Food, retail and stock belong with the tickets.Café, kitchen screens, gift shop, purchase orders and stock counts run on the same POS and guest record.
  • You want guest texting built in.Relay texts guests before, during and after the visit from inside the ticketing system.
  • You run on hardware your team knows.Elo terminals, Verifone readers, Microsoft Surface tablets and iPads.

Suite by suite

What each one is built for, from its own pages and its reviewers.

  • Gateway Galaxy

    The longest feature list of the five: admission control, resource management, group sales, retail, food and beverage, membership, reporting and CRM. Built for large parks and multi-site operators. Reviewers mention a learning curve and praise the support.

  • accesso

    Built for the big destinations: 1,100+ venues in 31 countries and $5 billion+ processed a year, with virtual queuing and a mobile app. Siriusware is now one of nine accesso products.

  • Centaman

    30+ years in attractions, with pages for zoos, aquariums and parks. Public reviews are few and old, so ask for references.

  • Tessitura

    The arts and culture platform: CRM, ticketing, fundraising and memberships on one system. Fits performing arts and museums with a development team. Reviewers say it takes training to use well.

  • Blackbaud Altru

    Ticketing, memberships and donations in one view. Fits museums and nonprofits that fundraise. Blackbaud reports 60% donation and 54% membership revenue increases for customers (its own figures).

RocketRez vs legacy suites: questions buyers ask

Does RocketRez do fundraising like Tessitura or Altru?

Not as a published module. RocketRez covers tickets, food, retail, memberships and guest texting. If donor CRM sits at the centre of your operation, Tessitura and Altru are built for it.

Is RocketRez a Centaman alternative?

Fossil Rim Wildlife Center moved from Centaman. Its Director of Tourism says the inventory management is much better and the move was one of the easiest transitions it has made.

Which hardware does RocketRez run on?

Elo touchscreen terminals, Verifone card readers, Microsoft Surface tablets, Apple iPads, cash drawers and handheld scanners.

How long does moving off a legacy suite take?

Typical implementation takes 8 to 12 weeks, planned back from your go-live date, with member and product data migrated and checked with you.
