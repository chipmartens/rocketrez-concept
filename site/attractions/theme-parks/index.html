Theme park software from RocketRez sells day tickets, season passes and ride bundles, and runs food, retail and guest texts on the same system. Rides scan QR and mobile tickets, so lines keep moving, and every sale shows up in one report. It replaces separate ticketing, pass and POS tools. Built by former attraction operators.

What operators ask us

Can RocketRez manage both tickets and season passes?

Yes. Day tickets, season passes, and ride bundles are all managed in one system with automated renewal workflows.

How does access control work across multiple attractions?

QR and mobile scanning works across rides, water parks, and VIP areas with configurable access rules.

Can I upsell bundles and add-ons at checkout?

Absolutely. Meal deals, VIP queues, and merch can be automatically offered during booking and post-purchase.

Will this help shorten ride lines?

Yes. High-speed scanning and centralized access control reduce bottlenecks and keep guests moving.
