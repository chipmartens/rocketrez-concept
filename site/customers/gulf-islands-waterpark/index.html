Home / Customers / Gulf Islands Waterpark

Hands-on training gave Gulf Islands Waterpark a smooth Memorial Day weekend.

Ahead of Memorial Day weekend, a RocketRez operator with 25 years in attractions set up the hardware and trained the park's staff on site.

Attraction
Waterpark

In this story

BeforeThe moveAfter

Inside the story

  • Guests flowed through entrances with minimal wait times
  • Staff handled transactions swiftly and resolved technical issues independently
  • EMV devices, scanners and hardware set up and tested before the rush

Before

Memorial Day weekend was close, and the park worried about its capacity to handle the holiday crowds. It asked RocketRez for hands-on help and training to get staff ready.

The move

Gina Cameron, a 25-year veteran operator, set up all the EMV devices, configured the scanners and made sure every piece of hardware was connected. She then ran intensive hands-on sessions on troubleshooting and best practices. Her recommendations: structured training before the season rush, standardized hardware with PIN-based login, and clear operational protocols.

“Seeing the transformation over those few days was incredible. From the moment I arrived to Memorial Day weekend, the team’s confidence and competence grew exponentially. It was a testament to their dedication and hard work.” Gina Cameron, Customer Operations Specialist, RocketRez

After

Staff who were unsure of complex situations became adept at managing high volumes of guests. Memorial Day weekend ran as one of the park's smoothest operations, with minimal waits at the entrances.
