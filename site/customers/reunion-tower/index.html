Reunion Tower dropped its professional photographer and sped up photo sales.

A 470-foot Dallas observation deck moved photo sales to on-site kiosks with RocketPhoto.

Attraction
Observation deck
Where
Dallas, Texas
Replaced
A previous photo system with a slow buying process and a professional photographer
Reunion Tower

In this story

BeforeThe moveAfter

Inside the story

  • Swift digital buying process at on-site kiosks
  • Retail items added to the kiosk buying process
  • No professional photographer needed, and less time spent editing photos

Before

Reunion Tower's Geo-Deck sits 470 feet up in downtown Dallas, a draw for field trips, anniversaries, private events and date nights, so photos matter. The previous system made guests scan a unique barcode for each photo, which slowed buying and cut conversion. A professional photographer on site can erode up to 75% of the margin on photos.

The move

Every general admission ticket includes a free digital photo, edited on site with no watermark. At the kiosk guests see paid options and retail such as frames, folders and a light-up frame, and can buy several photos with different backgrounds in one purchase.

“If I [Reunion Tower customer] want to buy Photo A, Photo B and Photo C, I can buy all three of those with different backgrounds and put them in different frames all in one purchase process.” Shawn Miller, Director of Operations

After

All of a guest's photos now appear on the kiosk from one barcode. The system needs little training or camera expertise, and the team gets answers from RocketRez within a couple of hours.

“When we signed up with RocketRez, we thought we’d get a vendor, but in reality we’ve found a helpful partnership where we can ask questions and regularly hear back from them within a couple of hours, not waiting days or weeks” Shon Stanbrough, IT Manager
