Date-specific tickets let Fresno Fright Nights raise Saturday prices.

An off-season water park became a Halloween haunt, and the team changed ticketing systems while launching the new business.

Attraction
Haunted attraction
Replaced
Previous POS provider, which could not do date-specific tickets or time slots

In this story

BeforeThe moveAfter

Inside the story

  • Training took a couple of weeks, with onboarding done alongside the launch
  • Same-day text messages offer cabanas, parking and food before guests arrive
  • Saturday prices raised and Friday discounts added, with guests held to their ticketed day

Before

Bob Martin spent 22 years in the water park industry and saw a facility that sits dormant eight months of the year. His team needed to hire 250 seasonal employees, and the old POS could not do date-specific tickets, tiered pricing, time slots or access controls. A Friday ticket still got a guest in on Saturday.

The move

They chose RocketRez for its flexibility, including an open API the previous provider never allowed, and for control over which day each ticket works. Training took a couple of weeks while the new business launched.

“RocketRez really opened the door technology-wise with the open API and really let us do what we wanted to do to make our business successful without having all these constraints on it.” Matt Massey, Chief Operating Officer, Fresno Fright Nights

After

Text messages to guests who bought that morning ask about cabanas, parking and food. Fresno Fright Nights uses a quarter of the park's footprint with four attractions today and plans eight within the next few years.

“RocketRez really helped us provide that control, which allowed me to raise my price on my Saturdays, put some discounts on Fridays, and I could know for sure that those guests were going to go on the right days.” Matt Massey, Chief Operating Officer, Fresno Fright Nights
