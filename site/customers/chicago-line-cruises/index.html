200 guests boarded in 15 minutes, by a summer crew.

A family-owned river cruise line replaced a ticketing system that took weeks to teach, and now boards 200 guests in 15 minutes.

Attraction
River architecture cruises
Where

Replaced

BeforeThe moveAfter

Inside the story

  • Up to 200 guests boarded in a 15-minute window
  • New staff learn the system in about ten minutes
  • Daily, weekly and monthly reports run by staff

Before

Chicago Line Cruises has run 90-minute architecture cruises, private events and charters on the Chicago River for over 40 years. Peak season runs from St. Patrick’s Day to Thanksgiving, and most of the ticket office is high school and college students hired for the summer.

The move

Chicago Line moved ticketing, POS and bookings onto RocketRez, with mobile tickets, scanning and connections to TripAdvisor, Viator and GetYourGuide. The team tested on a practice copy of the system before going live with customers.

“When we made the switch over, I learned the system in about ten minutes. Anyone could. It’s just user-friendly.” Elizabeth Johnson, Vice President

After

Ticket scanning lets the crew board up to 200 guests in a 15-minute window. Staff run daily, weekly and monthly reports on revenue and passengers themselves.

“There have been a few times when I needed customer support on a weekend. RocketRez got back to us right away, they called me on my cell phone.” Mia Alba, IT Consultant
