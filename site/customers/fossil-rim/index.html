65% more business with 55% less staffing, since launching with RocketRez in May 2020.

How a 1,900-acre wildlife park moved ticketing online, spread out its peak traffic and replaced a set of disjointed systems.

Inside the story

  • Ticketing moved online, with timed slots that spread the day
  • Staff scan pre-purchased tickets through car windows
  • Many days book to capacity, and still feel less busy

Before

Fossil Rim is a non-profit, founded in 1984, that breeds endangered and threatened species. Visitors drive a two-hour scenic route, stop at the Overlook Café and feed the animals. Revenue comes from lodging, tickets, events, the café and nature store, memberships and tours.

It could not sell tickets online. Vehicles arrived in floods at peak times, with two-hour waits, while off-peak hours sat quiet. Each department ran on its own system.

The move

Fossil Rim moved its whole ticketing process online, with timed slots that spread demand across the day. Staff scan pre-purchased tickets through car windows and see future sales before the weekend arrives, with a hands-on RocketRez support team behind them.

After

Many days now book to capacity, yet staff say it feels less busy, because guests book themselves and arrive across the day.

Fossil Rim has since recommended RocketRez to fellow members of its industry association.
