Yacht Starship automated dynamic pricing it used to track in a notebook.

Three boat brands in Tampa Bay and Clearwater replaced day-by-day manual price overrides with rule-based Demand-Based Pricing.

Attraction
Dining yacht and cruise fleet
Where
Tampa Bay and Clearwater, Florida
Replaced

Yacht Starship

BeforeThe moveAfter

Inside the story

  • Automation saves a tremendous amount of time
  • The operator keeps control through the rules she sets
  • Next: cart abandonment reminders through branded email templates

Before

Yacht Starship runs three brands, Yacht Starship, Pirate Water Taxi and The Lost Pearl, all on RocketRez. Operations Manager Jacquelyn Ketner raised prices by hand as boats neared sellout and tracked every price override in a big notebook.

The move

RocketRez Demand-Based Pricing runs on rules the operator inputs, and leaves control with the operator. It is built to scale the knowledge of tour operators rather than replace it, and still allows manual calls.

“We had to test it out, we didn’t know how the customer was going to react to our prices increasing, because we always had a standard price.” Jacquelyn Ketner, Operations Manager

After

Jacquelyn turned her knowledge into an operating rule, automated the work and moved on to the next growth opportunity. Next up is cart abandonment reminders sent through branded email templates.

“Dynamic Pricing has been phenomenal. Automation is saving me a tremendous amount of time. It gives me the opportunity to focus on other things.” Jacquelyn Ketner, Operations Manager
