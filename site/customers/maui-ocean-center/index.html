Home / Customers / Maui Ocean Center

Every Maui Ocean Center sale, online, on travel sites and at the gate, on one record.

On November 1, 2021 the aquarium moved its website, its online travel agent sales and in-person ticketing to RocketRez. Each transaction now posts to the right accounting bucket and reaches the CRM.

Inside the story

  • Launched November 1, 2021 with website, online travel agent and in-person ticketing from RocketRez
  • Each transaction posts revenue to the correct accounting bucket and integrates with the CRM
  • Next phase connects other Maui providers so offers can be bundled

Before

Maui Ocean Center wanted to be more than a one-day visit. Separate point solutions for online ticketing, point of sale and OTAs did not talk to each other, so the team could not save time, gather cross-functional data or bundle offers naturally.

The move

RocketRez now runs the center's website, online travel agent and in-person ticketing. With one source of truth, revenue lands in the right accounting bucket and syncs with the CRM, so leaders can see data they trust.

“Everything is integrated in one single platform. Eventually I would like to run the platform from a remote location, with my own KPI and metrics dashboard that I can follow. I think RocketRez is the piece of the puzzle that allows us to move our organization in that direction.” Tapani Vuori, General Manager

After

The next phase is connecting other experience providers in Maui so the center can bundle offers with them. Tapani Vuori also praised the RocketRez team's willingness to build custom work.

“I am impressed by [your] company and team’s willingness to be solutions driven. You tell us that, within reason, whatever you need, we'll make it happen.” Tapani Vuori, General Manager
