Home / Customers / Hemker Park and Zoo

From two systems and chaos to one, and a new leopard habitat.

A family-owned zoo replaced separate online and in-house ticketing, and turned the till into a conservation fund.

Attraction
Family-owned zoo
Where
Central Minnesota
Replaced
Two ticketing systems

In this story

BeforeThe moveAfter

Inside the story

  • Online booking and in-house sales on one system
  • Parties, feedings and events scheduled in one place
  • Round Up at the till helped fund a new leopard habitat

Before

Birthday parties, animal feedings and events were coordinated by hand across disconnected systems.

The move

Hemker moved online booking, in-house sales and memberships onto RocketRez, with scheduling for parties, feedings and events, and Round Up at every till.

“The support team has been great. They have been there to work with us. They call us. They’re there to assist us with whatever needs.” Jackie Poepping, Zoo Manager

After

Guests round up their purchases for conservation. That money helped build a new habitat for Koshka, an Amur leopard who needed a home when his California facility closed.

“Having that Roundup feature on our till systems so that our guests can be able to help out with our conservation mission here at the zoo has been a great impact.” Anne Blenker, Educational Director
