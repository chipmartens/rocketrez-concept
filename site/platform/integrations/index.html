Connected to the tools you already use.

Payments, travel sites, pricing, data and messaging, out of the box, so you keep the tools that already work. Need something else? Use the open API or Zapier.

StripePayments · NativeTakes payments online, at point of sale and on kiosks, including cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, ACH and Affirm. Guests get modern checkout; you get fast deposits, detailed reporting and built-in fraud prevention.PaymentsLive
Shift4PaymentsCard processingLive
PayPalPayments · NativeLets guests pay with their PayPal account at checkout, a trust signal that helps online conversion, especially with international visitors.PaymentsLive
SolaPayments · NativeSola (formerly CardKnox) processes online and on-site EMV terminal payments for attractions in North America, with chip and contactless cards and reporting across POS and online.PaymentsLive
Apple PayPayments · Included via StripeOne-tap checkout with iPhone or Apple Watch, online and at self-service kiosks, which helps mobile-first guests finish more purchases.PaymentsLive
Google PayPayments · Included via StripeFast mobile checkout for Android users at the online store and at kiosks, using cards already stored on the device.PaymentsLive
AffirmPayments · Included via StripeLets guests split larger purchases into monthly payments, which helps convert high-ticket bookings and lift average booking value.PaymentsLive
ChargeflowPayments · NativeChargeback and dispute management that monitors transactions, sends alerts and builds dispute responses with little effort from your team. you pay only when it recovers a disputed payment.PaymentsComing soon
ViatorDistribution · Direct, through RezdyList experiences on Viator through Rezdy and sync availability and pricing automatically, then manage Viator bookings inside RocketRez.OTAs & ResellersLive
GetYourGuideDistribution · Direct, through RezdySyncs tour and activity availability with GetYourGuide through Rezdy to capture more international bookings without manual updates.OTAs & ResellersLive
Google Things to DoDistribution · Direct, through RezdyPuts your tours and tickets in Google Maps, search results and travel panels with a Book Online button linking to your checkout, powered through Rezdy.OTAs & ResellersLive
CityPASSDistribution · NativeValidates CityPASS bundle tickets at the gate and consolidates redemption reporting, so you can join regional tourism programs.OTAs & ResellersLive
RezdyDistribution · NativeA channel manager built for tours and attractions that distributes your tickets and inventory to OTAs like Viator, GetYourGuide and Google Things to Do, with real-time availability sync.OTAs & ResellersLive
Travel CuriousDistribution · NativeTravel Curious (formerly Redeam) lets you scan third-party barcodes from OTAs, resellers and pass partners at your venue, with real-time validation and reconciliation.OTAs & ResellersLive
Power BIData · NativeConnects RocketRez data to Power BI so you can build custom dashboards for ticket sales, F&B, retail, attendance and marketing, including board-ready views.Power BILive
ZapierData · DirectConnects RocketRez to over 5,000 apps without code. A new web order or an abandoned cart can trigger actions in your other tools, like syncing orders to Google Sheets or updating a CRM.API AccessLive
SpotlioRevenue · NativeFlexible ticket pricing that responds to weather, dayparting and demand trends, so you do not update prices by hand every day.Dynamic PricingLive
Sensible WeatherRevenue · NativeEmbeds weather protection at checkout. Guests add weather insurance to a ticket and are compensated automatically if bad weather hits, which helps sell advance tickets.Dynamic PricingLive
TwilioMessaging · NativePowers SMS text messages to guests, from booking confirmations to visit reminders and schedule change notifications.RelayLive
Twilio SendGridMessaging · NativeSends transactional emails like ticket confirmations, schedule changes and receipts, with branded templates and deliverability at scale.RelayLive
DigonexRevenuePricing recommendations that use 10+ external factors like weather and hotel occupation rates, approved in the Digonex SEATS portal and synced to RocketRez. Digonex reviews performance and updates pricing every day.Dynamic PricingLive
