Every department’s numbers, the same day.

Attraction reporting in RocketRez is a built-in set of dashboards for ticketing, POS, food and drink, inventory and memberships, showing what is happening now. Reports go out on a schedule, and spend per guest is tracked by department. Every department works from the same numbers the same day, without exporting to spreadsheets.

  • See ticketing, POS, food and members together
  • Get reports sent on a schedule
  • Track spend per guest by department
Book a demoSee pricing

What changes with your dashboards

  • “We rebuild the same report in a spreadsheet every week.”

    Prebuilt dashboards and reports are ready on day one, with CSV and Excel export when you want your own cut.

  • “We learn what happened last week, not today.”

    Data refreshes hourly, so you can watch check-ins and adjust the day while it is still happening.

  • “Only the finance person can see the numbers.”

    Permission-based access controls which people and departments see which dashboards.

The day, hour by hour

Ticket scanning shows check-in rates by time, gate and ticket type. A headcount overview shows attendance by hour, day, ticket type and channel. Data refreshes hourly, so you can run the day from the dashboard, and use history to forecast attendance and plan staffing.

  • Ticket scanning dashboard
  • Headcount overview by hour, day, ticket type and channel
  • Hourly data refresh
  • Peak times to plan staffing and upselling

Revenue after discounts, comps and refunds

The net revenue dashboard shows what you earned after discounts, comps and refunds. Compare events, sales offices and channels, see which ticket types carry the most revenue, and track retail and food and beverage by product and location.

  • Net revenue dashboard
  • Ticket sales by event, channel, sales office and ticket type
  • Retail and F&B sales by product or location
  • Multi-location and department comparisons

Members: who renews and who lapses

Membership dashboards cover active memberships, average tenure, new members, renewals and expirations, plus memberships expiring by month and renewals against churn. Use them to start win-back campaigns before the lapse.

  • Active, new, renewed and expired memberships
  • Memberships expiring by month
  • Renewals compared with churn
  • Active memberships by type

Your data in your tools

Export to Excel or CSV for one-off reports. Connect Power BI, BI tools or your own dashboards through Power BI-ready datasets, API integrations and direct data access for analysts. Standard dashboards need no technical skill.

  • CSV and Excel export
  • Power BI-ready datasets
  • API integrations for BI and visualization tools
  • Direct data access for your analysts

What operators ask about your dashboards

Can I compare performance across locations or departments?

Yes. Multi-location operators can easily compare metrics across sites. You can drill down into performance by department (e.g., ticketing, retail, food and beverage) or staff member.

How often is data refreshed?

Data is refreshed hourly, giving you near-real-time insights into daily operations, ideal for managing check-ins, resolving staff issues, or adjusting on the fly.

Is Insights secure and permission-controlled?

Yes. Access is fully permission-based. Your team can control which users or departments see which dashboards or metrics
