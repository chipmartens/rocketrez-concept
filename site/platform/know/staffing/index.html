What operators ask about staff scheduling

Does RocketRez run payroll?

RocketRez builds a report from your employee timekeeping and sends it to your payroll provider, so staff are paid for the time they worked.

How do staff clock in?

With a web-based link on the POS or a mobile device, from their own profile.

Can the schedule follow ticket sales?

Yes. Scheduling connects to custom rules based on ticket sales and the tour schedule, so you can staff for the crowd you sold.
