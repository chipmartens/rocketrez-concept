Board reports built on your own data.

RocketRez connects to Microsoft Power BI, so you can build custom dashboards and KPIs on your own sales data. Use it for board reports, funder updates and grant reports, with tickets, food, retail and memberships in one data source. It sits next to the built-in Insights dashboards you use for day-to-day numbers.

What operators ask about Power BI reports

Do we need Power BI to see our numbers?

No. Insights includes prebuilt dashboards and reports. Power BI is for teams that want custom dashboards and deeper analysis.

Who sets it up?

Standard dashboards need no technical skill. For custom dashboards or Power BI, your IT team or a data analyst connects through RocketRez’s APIs.

Can we share dashboards with our board?

Yes. Power BI lets you share dashboards and reports across your team, and zoos use it for grant and board-ready reporting.
