What operators ask about reports and payments

What reports come built in?

Insights dashboards for ticketing, POS, food and drink, inventory and memberships, plus reports sent on a schedule.

Can we use our own reporting tools?

Yes. Connect Microsoft Power BI, use the open API, or connect 5,000+ apps through Zapier.

Who processes card payments?

Stripe, Shift4, PayPal or Sola, with Apple Pay, Google Pay, Affirm, gift cards and charge-to-room. Processing fees are shown up front.

Is RocketRez PCI-compliant?

Yes. Payments are PCI-compliant, with user permissions and audit logs.
