Take every payment, with every fee shown.

Attraction payment processing in RocketRez runs through Stripe, Shift4, PayPal or Sola, PCI-compliant, with every fee shown up front. Guests pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Affirm, gift card or charge to room, and Stripe Radar screens for fraud. Every payment lands in the same reports as your tickets, food and retail.

  • Process through Stripe, Shift4, PayPal or Sola
  • Accept wallets, gift cards and pay over time
  • Catch fraud with Stripe Radar

What changes with payments

  • “We find out about card fees when the statement arrives.”

    Every fee, including card processing, is shown up front in your quote.

  • “Online, the gate and the boat bar each settle differently.”

    Online, in-person and mobile payments land in one set of consolidated transaction data, which simplifies reconciliation and financial reporting.

  • “Fraud and chargebacks eat revenue.”

    Stripe Radar fraud tools screen card payments, and real-time transaction monitoring sends instant notifications.

Take payment anywhere on site

Accept payments online, in person and on mobile for tickets, merchandise and services. Payment processing is integrated with your existing flow, so the card reader takes the payment and the receipt closes the sale. Governors Island also accepts cash at its kiosks so guests without a card can still visit.

  • Online, in-person and mobile payments
  • Tickets, merchandise and services in one flow
  • Cash options where you need them

Pay the way guests prefer

Guests can pay by credit and debit card, mobile wallet, gift card or charge to room, and buy now and pay later with Affirm through Stripe. More ways to pay means fewer abandoned carts.

  • Credit and debit cards
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay
  • Affirm pay over time, via Stripe
  • Gift cards and charge to room

Fraud tools and clean books

PCI-compliant processing protects your business and your guests, with user permissions and audit logs. Stripe Radar adds fraud tools. Consolidated transaction data simplifies reconciliation and financial reporting.

  • PCI-compliant processing
  • Stripe Radar fraud tools
  • Real-time transaction monitoring
  • Consolidated transaction data

Choose the processor that fits

A network of payments partners gives you options: Stripe, Shift4, PayPal or Sola. Your rate is a line in your quote, so you know what you will pay before you sign.

  • Integrated with ticketing and POS reporting

What operators ask about payments

Which payment processors work with RocketRez?

Stripe, Shift4, PayPal and Sola.

What payment methods can guests use?

Credit and debit cards, mobile wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay, gift cards and charge to room, plus pay over time with Affirm through Stripe.

Are card fees shown up front?

RocketRez says it gives upfront visibility into all fees, including credit card processing. Your processing rate is a line in your quote.

How is card data protected?

Processing is PCI-compliant, and RocketRez includes user permissions, audit logs and secure payment processing.
