Turn every till into a donation box.

Round Up from RocketRez lets guests round any purchase up for your cause at the till. Supporters can also give monthly or annually, with receipts and thank-you emails sent for you. Hemker Park and Zoo used round-ups at the till to help fund a new leopard habitat.

  • Let guests round up any sale
  • Set up monthly or annual giving
  • Send receipts and thank-yous for you

What changes with Round Up and recurring giving

  • “Donations wait in a box by the exit.”

    Round Up asks every guest at the till and in the online checkout, so a donation is one tap on a sale they are already making.

  • “Giving only reaches the donors who go looking for it.”

    Guests round up to the nearest dollar or enter their own amount, on any purchase.

  • “Monthly supporters mean manual receipts and thank-you notes.”

    Recurring monthly or annual giving sends receipts and a thank-you template email for you.

Every till becomes a donation point

Round Up appears at the point of sale and in your online checkout. Guests round their purchase up to the nearest dollar or enter a custom amount. Hemker Park and Zoo says having Round Up on the tills has had a great impact on its conservation mission.

  • Round Up at the POS and online checkout
  • True nearest-dollar round up or a custom amount
  • Custom imagery and descriptions at the till for your cause

Different charities at different terminals

Round Up is set up as a retail item, so different GL codes can point to different charities. Turn it on or off by terminal, or show a different charity at each one. Ask for the gift where it makes sense.

  • Several GL codes tied to different charities
  • On or off by POS terminal
  • A different charity at each terminal

Spare change that builds a habitat

Hemker Park and Zoo guests helped fund a state-of-the-art habitat for Koshka, an Amur leopard who needed a new home, through Round Up. Small amounts add up when every sale offers the choice.

  • Guest donations tied to a named project
  • Total dollars raised as the number to track
  • Donations in the same reports as tickets, food and retail

Recurring giving with receipts built in

Museums and non-profits can take online donations and memberships that recur monthly, or annually with a discount. Renewal confirmations and receipts go out automatically, along with a template email that carries your thanks and periodic updates.

  • Monthly or annual recurring payments
  • Automatic receipts and renewal confirmations
  • Template thank-you emails

What operators ask about Round Up and recurring giving

Where can guests round up?

At the point of sale and in your online checkout, on any purchase.

Can we raise money for more than one cause?

Yes. Round Up is set up as a retail item, so you can tie different GL codes to different charities, and turn it on or off by terminal or show a different charity at each one.

Can guests give their own amount?

Yes. It can be a true nearest-dollar round up, or guests can enter a custom donation amount.

Can donors give every month?

Yes. Museums such as Mennonite Heritage Village take recurring payments monthly, or annually with a discount, with receipts and renewal confirmations sent automatically.
