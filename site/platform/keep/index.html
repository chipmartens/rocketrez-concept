What operators ask about memberships and repeat visits

Can members renew without calling us?

Yes. Memberships renew on their own, monthly or annually, with reminders and receipts sent for you.

Where do member perks work?

At the gate, the café, the shop and online, applied without staff looking anything up.

What is Relay?

Relay is the guest texting tool built into RocketRez ticketing. It sends confirmations, add-on offers and day-of updates, and has a two-way inbox.

Can families bring guests on a season pass?

Yes. Staff redeem the passholder and their guests in one scan.
