Memberships that renew on their own.

Membership and season pass software from RocketRez lets you design tiers for families and individuals, send digital member cards with QR codes, and apply perks at the till and online. Renewal reminders go out automatically, and dashboards track attendance, renewals and churn, so memberships renew on their own instead of by hand.

  • Design tiers for families and individuals
  • Send digital cards with QR codes
  • Remind and renew automatically

What changes with memberships

  • “Renewals mean chasing members by email and phone.”

    Set reminders and let members renew in the My Account portal or at the POS. Mennonite Heritage Village called renewals and member communication a strain before RocketRez.

  • “A member’s perks work at the gate but not at the café or the shop.”

    Memberships sync across ticketing, retail and POS, so benefits apply on the spot at every till and online.

  • “We cannot see who is about to lapse.”

    Dashboards show active, renewed, expired and expiring memberships by month, with renewals against churn.

Tiers that fit every household

Build Basic, Premium and Family tiers with their own benefits and lengths, set in days, months or years to match your operating calendar. Targeted perks, such as exclusive access, discounts and event invitations, give guests a reason to move up.

  • Flexible levels for individuals and families
  • Custom durations in days, months or years
  • Perks and invitations by tier
  • Membership designs that match your brand

Perks that work at every till

A member is a member everywhere. Memberships activate immediately and member pricing applies in real time at the POS and online. Issue a printed card at the till or a digital card on the guest’s phone, with a QR code to scan in.

  • Instant activation
  • Real-time member discounts at the POS and online
  • Print or digital member cards
  • Sell, renew or upgrade at the POS or on your website

Renewals that run themselves

Members renew in the My Account portal or at the POS, and can upgrade at any time by replacing, extending or scheduling the new term. Reminders keep memberships active, and Relay sends confirmations, renewal reminders and member offers by text and email.

  • Self-service member portal for renewals and upgrades
  • Renewal reminders
  • Mid-term upgrades
  • Relay messages for confirmations and offers

See retention before it drops

Membership reports show active members, average tenure, new members, renewals and expirations. Usage tracking across locations flags the members ready for an upgrade, and a combined view of membership, ticket and retail activity feeds your marketing.

  • Active, new, renewed and expired memberships
  • Memberships expiring by month, and renewals against churn
  • Usage tracking across locations
  • Automated upsell prompts

What operators ask about memberships

How can guests renew their memberships?

Guests can renew memberships through the My Account portal on your website or at the POS by selecting their membership type and completing the purchase.

Can memberships be upgraded mid-term?

Yes, guests can upgrade their memberships at any time, choosing to replace the current term, extend it, or set the upgrade to activate upon expiration.

How do memberships integrate with ticketing and retail?

Membership benefits, such as discounts or loyalty rewards, are automatically applied during purchases in all sales channels.
