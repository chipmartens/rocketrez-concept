Stock that stays right in every shop.

Gift shop inventory in RocketRez tracks stock in every shop on the same system as the till. Order from vendors with purchase orders, receive deliveries by barcode, transfer stock between locations, log losses and scan UPCs. Counts stay right across the gift shop, the kiosk and the café, so you reorder before a bestseller runs out.

  • Order from vendors with purchase orders
  • Receive deliveries by barcode
  • Transfer stock between locations

What changes with shop stock

  • “Receiving stock means typing product codes one line at a time.”

    Scan UPCs to find products and receive by barcode, or add many products from one vendor to a purchase order in one step.

  • “Shrinkage is a number nobody can explain.”

    Log every loss with a reason and a note, and run reports so each missing item has an owner.

  • “The shop count and the books never match.”

    Run full or partial counts with a scanner, track variances in real time and approve them in bulk.

Find and receive stock by scanning

Scan a UPC instead of typing it, and the product is there. Receive shipments by scanning barcodes or entering products by hand. USB barcode scanners work across purchase orders, transfers, loss logging and receiving.

  • UPC-based lookup and quick search by name or SKU
  • Barcode receiving
  • Bulk item selection on purchase orders
  • USB barcode scanners and manual entry

Stock that moves between locations

Move stock between sales offices with a scan-and-transfer process and keep tabs on levels across every location. Gift shop managers, retail staff and operators all see the same numbers.

  • Scan-and-transfer between locations
  • Stock-level visibility across sales offices
  • Advanced filtering by location, variance, cost or status

Counts that reconcile and losses that explain themselves

Run physical counts with barcode scanning and see variances as they happen. Log every loss with a reason and notes, then use reports to talk about shrinkage with real data.

  • Full or partial physical counts
  • Real-time variance tracking and resolution
  • Loss logging with reasons and notes
  • Bulk product actions across filtered lists

Vendors, orders and costs in one place

Keep supplier details, purchase orders and order history together so reordering is quick. Receive against dockets that link to purchase orders and invoices, and record backorders and rejections. Edit product costs directly for accurate margins.

  • Vendor and purchase history
  • Docket-based receiving linked to purchase orders and invoices
  • Cost management tools
  • Insights dashboards for retail sales by category and product

What operators ask about shop stock

Does the barcode scanner work for all inventory processes?

Absolutely! Barcode scanning is supported across purchase orders, loss logging, inventory transfers, and product receiving, wherever you need it most.

Can I add multiple products at once when creating a purchase order?

Yes! Instead of adding products one by one, you can select multiple items from the same vendor and add them in bulk.

Does this feature work with external scanning hardware?

Yes! RocketRez Inventory Management supports USB barcode scanners and other compatible scanning devices, making inventory management even more.
