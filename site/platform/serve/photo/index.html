Sell the souvenir photo with the ticket.

The RocketRez photo system sells souvenir photos with the ticket. Run a photo kiosk at the exit, link every photo to the guest’s ticket, and print or share on the spot. Photo sales count toward what each guest spends, in the same reports as admissions. Reunion Tower in Dallas runs its photo sales on it.

  • Run a photo kiosk at the exit
  • Link every photo to the guest’s ticket
  • Print and share on the spot

What changes with souvenir photo sales

  • “Guests scan a barcode for every single photo, then walk away.”

    One barcode brings every photo up on the kiosk screen, so guests pick several and buy them in one purchase.

  • “A professional photographer eats the photo margin.”

    Reunion Tower no longer needs one. Staff capture and edit photos without specialist skills, in less time.

  • “Photo sales run on a separate system from the ticket.”

    Photos link to the guest’s ticket and sell online, in person or at kiosks, and the sales land in the same reports.

Sell the photo with the ticket

Each photo links to the guest’s ticket and sits in an online gallery they can open anytime. Sell it online, in person or at a self-serve kiosk, and it lands in the same reports as admissions, food and retail.

  • Ticket-linked photos in an online gallery
  • Sales online, in person and at kiosks
  • Photos in the same reports as everything else

Let guests build their own order

At the Reunion Tower kiosk a guest can buy photo A, B and C with different backgrounds and frames in one purchase. The kiosk shows photos, backgrounds and upgrades, and retail items join the same buying flow.

  • Self-serve kiosk with background choices and upgrades
  • Several photos in different frames in one purchase
  • Retail items added to the kiosk flow

Keepsakes and shareable files

Printed photos give guests something to take home. Digital downloads give them something to post. Capture high-resolution photos and customize backgrounds, sizes and upgrade options to match your brand.

  • Printed photos for keepsakes
  • Digital downloads for sharing
  • Custom backgrounds, sizes and upgrades

A free photo that leads to a sale

Reunion Tower gives every general admission guest a complimentary digital photo, a low-cost marketing channel when guests share it. Collecting the photo at the kiosk puts paid options and retail items in front of them at the same moment.

  • Complimentary digital photo with admission
  • Paid options, frames and folders at the kiosk
  • Photos edited on site by staff, with no photographer on payroll

What operators ask about souvenir photo sales

How do guests find their photos?

Each photo links to the guest’s ticket in an online gallery. At Reunion Tower, one barcode brings every photo up at the kiosk.

Can a guest buy several photos in one order?

Yes. At the Reunion Tower kiosk a guest can buy several photos with different backgrounds and frames in one purchase.

Do we need a professional photographer or editing skills?

Reunion Tower’s director of operations says the system needs very little training or camera expertise, and the photos upload at a quality that cuts editing time. The tower no longer needs a professional photographer.

Can guests take printed photos and digital ones?

Both. Guests can take a printed keepsake or a digital download ready to share online.
