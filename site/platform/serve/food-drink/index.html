Feed the crowd and count it as guest spend.

Food and beverage POS from RocketRez runs quick-service counters, full-service restaurants with table management and kitchen screens, catering and VIP packages on the same system as the ticket. Meal combos pre-sell with admission, and every order counts toward what each guest spends, so the café shows up in the same report as the gate.

  • Run quick service and full service
  • Send orders to kitchen screens
  • Pre-sell meal combos with tickets

What changes with food and drink

  • “The café and the gift shop run on different systems from the gate.”

    Tickets, food and retail sell from one POS, with reporting for all three in one place.

  • “Kitchen orders get lost between the till and the line.”

    Orders placed at the POS go straight to the kitchen display, or print as tickets.

  • “Food and merchandise only get sold after the guest is inside.”

    Pre-sell meals, VIP packages and catering add-ons with the ticket, and bundle souvenirs with admission.

Quick service to full service, one till

Food stands, sit-down restaurants and the gift shop all sell from the same POS, so staff learn one tool and managers read one report. Run concessions, table service, VIP packages and event catering across multiple venues.

  • Quick-service and concession checkouts
  • Table management for full-service restaurants
  • VIP packages and event catering
  • Multi-venue coordination

Kitchen screens and tableside ordering

Servers take orders and payment on a mobile device at the table. Orders show on the kitchen display, or print as tickets, so the line sees what is next and guests wait less.

  • Kitchen display system
  • Mobile and tableside POS
  • Mobile POS over Wi-Fi or mobile data
  • Every purchase syncs to inventory

A gift shop that sells with the ticket

Combine tickets with souvenirs or apparel to build package deals at checkout. Members get their discounts automatically, and you can sell retail online alone or bundled with tickets, with promo codes and saved payment details.

  • Gift shop bundling with tickets
  • Membership offers applied at checkout
  • Online store with promo codes and saved payment information
  • Dynamic reporting permissions by role or department

Know what sells down to the last bite

Track every item you sell, from a bite to a souvenir. Real-time stock levels prevent shortages and overstock, vendor tracking tells you when to reorder, and sales reports across ticketing, retail and dining help forecast demand.

  • Automated stock tracking
  • Vendor and purchase order management
  • Sales insights across ticketing, retail and dining

What operators ask about food and drink

Can I manage both retail and restaurant operations in one system?

Yes! RocketRez integrates food, retail, and ticketing into one POS platform.

How does the Kitchen Display System (KDS) work?

Orders placed at the POS automatically send to the kitchen for preparation, either displayed digitally or printed as tickets.

Can I upsell food & retail items at checkout?

Yes! Bundle meals, drinks, and merchandise with ticket sales to maximize per-guest spend.
