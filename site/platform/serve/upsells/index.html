Offer the add-on at every step of the visit.

Upsells in RocketRez offer the add-on at every step of the visit: in the cart at checkout, by text before the visit through automated offer workflows, and as bundles at the till. Cabanas, lockers, meals and fast-lane passes sell before guests reach the gate, and each one counts toward what that guest spends.

  • Offer add-ons in the cart
  • Send offers by text before the visit
  • Add bundles at the till

What changes with add-on offers

  • “The add-on gets offered at the till, after the guest decided what to spend.”

    Offer parking, feedings, meals and upgrades in the cart, before the guest arrives.

  • “Nobody is selling during the visit.”

    Automated offers trigger before, during and after the visit by text, based on what the guest already bought.

  • “Slow items and quiet days need a reason to sell.”

    Promo codes, bundles and gift certificates give guests one, and reports show which ones work.

Add-ons in the cart

Offer VIP access, an exclusive tour, a meal package or a photo while the guest is still deciding. Upsell upgrades and cross-sell extras in an online cart that feels like any other shop, then offer the rest at the point of sale.

  • Upsells for premium experiences and private upgrades
  • Cross-sells for meals, merchandise and photos
  • Cart that feels natural to guests
  • Offers at checkout and at the POS

Bundles and packages in one purchase

Sell tickets, retail items and parking as one bundle so guests get what they need to enjoy the day. Build themed packages that combine event access and retail items for event attendees and private charters.

  • Product bundles for tickets, retail and parking
  • Themed packages for events and charters
  • Multi-attraction ticket bundles

Offers that send themselves

Use guest data to recommend the most appealing offer, and set automated promotions that trigger before, during and after the experience. Text messages and chat bots carry them, and reporting shows what converts.

  • Automated offers before, during and after the visit
  • SMS chat bots for offers
  • Recommendations based on guest data
  • Reporting to tune your strategy

Promos, credit and gift certificates

Promo codes move stagnant or seasonal items and power flash sales. Gift certificates pre-sell revenue and let guests give your experience as a gift. Store credit keeps refund value with your attraction, and gratuity lets guests tip on an order or bill.

  • Promo codes and flash sales
  • Gift certificates
  • Store credit for ticket refunds
  • Gratuity on orders and bills

What operators ask about add-on offers

Where can guests see upsell offers?

In the online checkout cart, at the point of sale, and by text before, during and after the visit through Relay.

What can we bundle together?

Tickets, retail items, parking and food as a single bundle, or themed packages that combine event access and retail for attendees and private charters.

Can we give refunds as credit?

Yes. Ticket refunds can be issued as store credit so the guest still gets to experience your attraction.

Do promo codes and gift certificates work in the same system?

Yes. Promo codes support flash sales and slow-moving items, and gift certificates pre-sell revenue.
