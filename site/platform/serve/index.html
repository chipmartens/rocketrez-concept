What operators ask about food, retail and photo

Which hardware works with RocketRez?

Elo touchscreen terminals, Verifone card readers, Microsoft Surface tablets, Apple iPads, cash drawers and handheld scanners.

Does the POS work away from the building?

Yes. Mobile POS runs over Wi-Fi or 4G, so staff can sell at a kiosk, a cart or the dock.

Can members get their discount at the café?

Yes. Member perks apply at the gate, the café and the shop, without staff looking anything up.

Can guests donate at the till?

Yes. Guests can round up any sale for your cause, and it shows in the same reports as everything else.
