Timed tickets that keep the gate line short.

Timed entry ticketing from RocketRez sells timed slots, all-day passes, multi-stop tours and season passes online, at the gate and by phone, with capacity you set per slot. Guests reschedule themselves, and staff scan QR or print-at-home tickets at the gate. Every channel sells from one live inventory, so the gate line stays short.

  • Spread guests across the day with timed slots
  • Let guests reschedule without calling you
  • Scan QR or print-at-home tickets at the gate

What changes with timed tickets

  • “We sell the same boat on three systems and one of them oversells.”

    Online, the gate, phone, travel sites and resellers draw from one live ticket inventory. No manual allocations and no end-of-day reconciliation.

  • “Everyone shows up at opening and the line is out the door.”

    Timed tickets with a capacity per slot spread guests across the day, and you can see when they are coming before they arrive.

  • “A guest calls to change a date and someone refunds and rebooks by hand.”

    Guests reschedule themselves in a branded mobile flow. Your pricing, inventory and refund rules apply automatically, and refunds or pay-the-difference happen without staff.

One live inventory behind every sale

Whatever the guest buys on, the seat count is the same. Online, point of sale, phone, travel sites and resellers pull from one live inventory, so nobody oversells and nobody reconciles at close. Set up each boat, exhibit, theatre, school tour or charter as its own event from one central console.

  • Real-time inventory sync across online, POS, phone, OTA and reseller channels
  • Custom capacity limits and reserved inventory per slot
  • Live online calendars guests book from
  • Separate events by boat, exhibit, theatre, group, educational tour or charter

Shorter lines at opening time

Timed entry spreads the crowd and scanning gets the rest through the door. Staff scan QR codes or print-at-home tickets on a handheld or a phone and know in one scan whether a ticket is good. Chicago Line Cruises boards 200 guests in 15 minutes. Fossil Rim moved ticketing online with allocated time slots and has done 65% more business with 55% less staffing since launching in May 2020.

  • Timed ticketing to spread guests through the day
  • Capacity controls that stop sales at the number you set
  • Handheld and mobile scanning, contactless or paper
  • QR codes or print-at-home tickets for guests

Every ticket type you really sell

Timed tickets, all-day passes, multi-leg trips, season passes and pre-paid passes live in one system. Custom rules collect visitor details and show the right options at the right step, and the cart bundles tickets with memberships, dining and retail.

  • Timed, general admission, all-day and multi-leg tickets
  • Season passes and multi-pass options
  • Custom rules for visitor information and ticket options
  • Bundles of tickets, memberships, dining and retail in one cart

Guests change their own bookings

Guests move a date, a time or a quantity without a phone call. Availability and price update the moment they change, and refunds or pay-the-difference run on your rules. It suits operators who allow booking changes and refundable tickets.

  • Self-service rescheduling in a mobile flow that matches your brand
  • Real-time inventory and pricing when a guest changes an order
  • Automatic refunds or pay-the-difference without staff
  • Fewer support calls, cancellations and no-shows

What operators ask about timed tickets

What types of tickets can I offer with RocketRez?

You can offer a wide range of ticket types, including timed tickets, general admission, all-day tickets, pre-paid passes, multi-leg tickets, and multi-pass options to suit your operation’s unique needs.

How does RocketRez support ticket sales across multiple channels?

RocketRez offers a unified platform that synchronizes inventory and integrates with web, mobile, POS, kiosk, and OTA channels.

Can RocketRez support high-volume ticket sales during peak times?

Yes, our cloud-based infrastructure ensures reliability during high-traffic periods.
