Timed tickets that keep the gate line short

Timed entry spreads guests across the day, so the gate line stays short. Sell all-day passes, multi-stop tickets and season passes too, and let guests reschedule themselves instead of calling you.

  • Live calendars with capacity per slot
  • Guests reschedule on their own
  • QR and print-at-home tickets

Prices that rise on busy days and fill the quiet ones

Set the rule once and the price moves on its own: early bird, last ten days, last hour. Or use the built-in demand pricing, or pricing partners Spotlio and Digonex.

  • First 30 tickets at one price, then the next
  • Rise in the last days before an event
  • Drop an hour before it starts

Groups that book themselves

School trips, corporate outings, charters and buyouts book online, with quotes, e-signatures and invoices sent for you. No more phone tag.

  • Custom quotes and deposits
  • E-signatures on the contract
  • Invoices sent for you

What operators ask about tickets and booking

Which travel sites can sell our tickets?

Viator, GetYourGuide, Google Things to Do, Klook, TripAdvisor, Expedia and CityPASS. Viator and GetYourGuide are direct integrations, connected through Rezdy, and Travel Curious redeems reseller barcodes. They all sell from the same calendar as your website and gate.

Can guests change their own booking?

Yes. Guests reschedule in a self-service portal instead of calling you.

How does dynamic pricing work?

Three ways: built-in demand-based pricing, your own rules and overrides, or a pricing partner such as Spotlio or Digonex.

Can we sell season passes and multi-stop tickets?

Yes. Timed tickets, all-day passes, multi-leg trips and season passes all run on the same system.
