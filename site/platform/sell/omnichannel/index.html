One calendar for every place you sell.

Omnichannel sales means your website, the gate, the phone, kiosks and travel sites all sell from one inventory. In RocketRez every channel draws from the same calendar and seat count, so a sale in one place updates every other place at once. Nothing oversells at peak, and you see every channel’s sales together.

  • Sell the same seats on every channel
  • Stop overselling at peak
  • See every channel’s sales together

What changes with one calendar for every channel

  • “Every channel has its own login, its own count and its own report.”

    Web, gate, phone, kiosk and travel sites sit in one system, with one availability and one set of reports.

  • “Walk-up guests and online guests compete for the same last seats.”

    Availability updates in real time across every platform, which cuts the risk of overbooking.

  • “We only know how guests buy on one channel.”

    Bring every channel into one record and see how guests behave across them, then market to what they do.

One seat count for every channel

Sell in person, by phone, at kiosks, on your website and through resellers and travel sites, all from the same inventory. Ticket availability synchronizes in real time across every platform, so the last seat is only sold once.

  • In-person, phone, kiosk, web and OTA sales on one inventory
  • Real-time availability across all platforms
  • Reseller and OTA channels on the same live count
  • One set of reports for every channel

Sell where the guest already is

Some guests buy ahead online, some at a kiosk on the day and some from a person. Governors Island sells online, by phone and at four self-serve kiosks, with agents free to answer questions, to roughly 2,000 visitors on a weekday and up to 20,000 on a weekend.

  • Consistent prices and rules on every channel
  • Kiosks next to agents and online sales
  • Phone sales with the same inventory
  • Guests can switch channels without friction

See which channel does the work

When every sale lands in one system you can see which channel brings revenue, which guests buy which tickets and where to put marketing money. Better data from every touchpoint drives better marketing decisions.

  • Sales by channel and sales office
  • Customer behaviour and preferences across touchpoints
  • Insights dashboards covering every channel

What operators ask about one calendar for every channel

Which channels share one inventory?

Online, point of sale, phone, kiosks, travel sites (OTAs) and resellers all draw from the same live availability.

Will two channels sell the last seat at the same time?

RocketRez synchronizes ticket availability in real time across every platform to cut the risk of overbooking.

Can we limit how much one channel can sell?

Yes. You can set allocation limits per channel or prioritize inventory for direct sales.

Do kiosks sell from the same inventory as everything else?

Yes. Self-serve kiosks sell from the same inventory as online and POS sales. Governors Island runs four kiosks at the ticket booth alongside online sales, phone sales and agents.
