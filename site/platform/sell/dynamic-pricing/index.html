Charge more on busy days. Fill the quiet ones.

Dynamic pricing for attractions charges more on busy days and less on quiet ones. RocketRez gives you three ways to do it: demand-based pricing built in, your own rules and price overrides, or a pricing partner like Spotlio or Digonex. Yacht Starship used it to replace day-by-day manual price overrides across three boat brands.

  • Price by demand, built in
  • Set your own rules and overrides
  • Plug in Spotlio or Digonex
Book a demoSee pricing

What changes with demand pricing

  • “We charge the same price on a sold-out Saturday and a quiet Tuesday.”

    Rules based on ticket volume or time raise the price as a slot fills. You set them, and you can override them any time.

  • “Raising prices by hand is a full-time job.”

    Yacht Starship’s operations manager used to change prices day by day as boats neared sell-out. Pricing rules now do that work.

  • “Quiet weekdays stay quiet.”

    Show the cheaper days right on the booking calendar, so guests see the deal at a glance and move to the days you need to fill.

Price with demand and keep control

Pick how hands-on you want to be. RocketRez Demand-Based Pricing is built in, with no third-party contract. Rules-based pricing follows ticket volume or time. Spotlio and Digonex plug in when you want a pricing engine. Capture more at peak and fill seats when demand dips.

  • Built-in Demand-Based Pricing, ready out of the box
  • Rules based on ticket volume or time
  • Manual price overrides
  • Spotlio and Digonex integrations

Guests see the price and trust it

Dynamic prices only work if guests are not surprised by them. The booking engine shows “starting at” prices on times and calendars, prompts a guest to refresh if a price changes while they sit in the cart, and shows rate alerts. Fewer pricing complaints is the point.

  • “Starting at” pricing on times and calendars
  • Price check at checkout when a rate changes
  • Calendar-based rate display across dates
  • Tickets left and amount saved display with Spotlio

Rules that fit your operation

Boat tours know the weather can send demand to zero or fill three boats from walk-ups. Operators set the rule once and let it run. Tour rate mapping connects each rule to the right ticket types and events, so the right prices land on the right tours.

  • Rules by ticket volume or time
  • Tour rate mapping to ticket types and events
  • Works at the POS with native pricing and Digonex
  • Supports general admission, timed entry and all-day passes

Bring in an economist if you want one

Digonex uses ten or more outside factors, including weather and hotel occupancy, to recommend prices daily. You approve recommendations in the Digonex portal and they sync into RocketRez. Spotlio adjusts prices in real time on the web, with upper and lower limits you set.

  • Digonex daily price optimization
  • Recommendations approved by you, then synced
  • Spotlio real-time pricing for your web engine
  • Promotional pricing by demand, time of day or campaign

What operators ask about demand pricing

Will my guests be confused by changing prices?

Not with RocketRez. We’ve designed the guest experience to be clear and transparent, with “starting at” pricing, calendar views, and alerts if prices change during checkout.

Can I use Dynamic Pricing at the Point of Sale (POS)?

Yes, with our native pricing and Digonex integration. Spotlio is currently web-only.

Do I need to use a third-party to get started?

Not necessarily. You can use RocketRez Demand-Based Pricing right out of the box, no third-party contracts or setup required.
