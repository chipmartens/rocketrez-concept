Customizable checkout from RocketRez is a booking page that matches your own site. Set your colours with the web sales editor, customise the date picker, and offer add-ons in the cart. The same checkout sells on desktop, phones and kiosks, and every booking lands in RocketRez with the rest of your sales.

What changes with your checkout

  • “Our booking page looks nothing like our website, and guests notice.”

  • “Guests drop out halfway through the booking.”

A booking page that looks like yours

Guests stay on a page that feels like your own site, so they trust it enough to pay. Match brand colours and fonts, take full CSS and HTML control, and choose whether the booking engine embeds in your page or opens as a pop-up. Online sales keep your domain, your SEO value and your guests’ trust.

  • Brand matching for colours and fonts
  • Full CSS and HTML control over the look
  • Embed in your site or open as a pop-up
  • Online sales on your own domain

Fewer steps between browsing and paid

A checkout that looks like every other shop guests use gets finished more often. The booking flow is built with the usual online shopping tools, and you can find and fix the steps where guests stall. Add-ons, bundles and a live cart sit in the same flow.

  • Streamlined checkout flow with the standard eCommerce tools
  • Add-ons and bundles in the cart
  • Calendar that shows price differences across dates
  • Usability and navigation tuned for conversion

The same checkout on your kiosks

Run the web engine in kiosk mode and guests buy from the same checkout at a self-serve kiosk. Governors Island put four kiosks at the ticket booth so an agent is not needed at midnight on a weekday, and agents spend their time answering questions instead.

  • Kiosk mode for self-serve kiosks
  • Same inventory and prices as online and POS
  • Custom options for your own rules, such as accepting cash at a kiosk

Change it without calling a developer

Museums and tours rotate what they sell. The drag and drop web engine takes new images and descriptions as often as you need, with capacity and price settings managed centrally for each new event. Mennonite Heritage Village had people buying tickets within a couple of hours of going live.

  • Drag and drop modules for new exhibits, events and tours
  • Centrally managed capacity and pricing
  • Rich imagery and descriptions for every offer

What operators ask about your checkout

Can the booking page match our website?

Yes. Each web engine can match your colours and fonts, and you get full CSS and HTML control over the look.

Do guests have to leave our site to book?

No. Embed the booking engine on your own pages or open it as a pop-up. Online sales run from your own website, which keeps your domain, your SEO value and your guests’ trust.

Can we use the same checkout on a self-serve kiosk?

Yes. The web engine runs in kiosk mode, so a kiosk sells from the same inventory as online and the point of sale.

How hard is it to add a new exhibit or event?

Mennonite Heritage Village updates images and descriptions for rotating exhibits through the drag and drop web engine, with capacity and pricing set centrally. It takes minimal technical knowledge.
