    Quick Start Guide

    Step 1: Plan Your Campaign

    Choose the template that best fits your goal:

    1. Gift with Purchase: Build value without discounting
    2. Major Discount: Drive high-volume bookings
    3. Season Pass Special: Encourage repeat visits
    4. BOGO Offer: Increase group bookings

    Step 2: Coordinate Your Channels

    1. Customize your social media post
    2. Schedule it to go live 1-2 hours before your email
    3. Use consistent messaging and deadlines across both

    Step 3: Pre-Launch Checklist

    Essential items to check before sending:

    • All [placeholder] text is replaced
    • Links and CTAs work
    • Dates and deadlines are correct
    • Mobile preview looks good

    Pro Tips for Success:

    • Timing: Send emails early morning (9-10am) for best results
    • Urgency: Always include clear end dates for offers
    • Mobile: Most bookings happen on phones, always check mobile view
    • Follow-up: Plan to send a reminder email 24-48 hours before offer ends

    Make It Your Own

    Here are some simple ways to customize:

    • Add your attraction photos
    • Use your brand colors
    • Include your logo
    • Adjust copy to match your voice
