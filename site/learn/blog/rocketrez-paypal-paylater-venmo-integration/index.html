    Introducing Our New Partnership with PayPal

    At RocketRez, we’re always on the lookout for ways to enhance the services we provide to our clients in the tours and attractions industry. We understand the challenges you face, particularly when it comes to providing smooth and reliable payment options to your customers. That’s why we are thrilled to announce our new partnership with PayPal, one of the leading global payment platforms, which includes Pay Later and Venmo options.

    The Challenge: Simplifying Payments in a Complex World

    For businesses in the tours and attractions sector, the ability to offer straightforward, secure, and versatile payment options isn't just a convenience, it's a necessity. Today’s customers expect flexibility and ease in their transactions, whether they’re booking an aquarium visit, a boat tour, or a safari adventure. The challenge has always been how to integrate such solutions without complicating the payment process or sacrificing security.

    PayPal, Pay Later, and Venmo Help Improve Guest Experiences

    PayPal is renowned for its robust security measures and is trusted by millions of users worldwide. By integrating PayPal with RocketRez, we are enabling you to offer a payment method that is both familiar and reliable to your customers.

    With the addition of PayPal Pay Later, your customers can now enjoy more flexibility in how they manage their finances. With PayPal's buy-now pay-later, guests can book their next adventure with you immediately and pay over time, making larger family outings or exclusive events more accessible, all while you get paid upfront.

    Venmo brings a socially savvy twist to transactions, favored particularly by younger consumers who appreciate the ease and informality of mobile payments. Offering Venmo as a payment option not only simplifies the transaction process but also taps into a key demographic that values quick and easy ways to split costs among friends.

    How does this help Tour and Attraction Operators?

    By integrating these PayPal services, RocketRez is providing a seamless and secure payment integration directly into your booking system. This means faster checkout processes, reduced cart abandonment, and a more flexible payment environment.

    Adding Value to Our Customers

    This partnership is about more than just adding payment options. It’s about providing you with the tools to grow and adapt in an ever-evolving market. With PayPal, Pay Later, and Venmo, you can expect:

    • Increased Conversion Rates and Average Order Value: By minimizing friction in the payment process.
    • Enhanced Customer Satisfaction: Through reliable and flexible payment solutions.
    • Broader Market Reach: Attract younger audiences with Venmo and offer budget-friendly solutions with Pay Later.

    Our goal at RocketRez is to ensure that you, our valued clients, have the best tools at your disposal to succeed. This new partnership with PayPal is a significant step forward in that direction.

    Quick Stats:

    • 46% higher checkout conversion with PayPal (source).
    • 33% of Americans use PayPal for online payments (source). The majority of Paypal’s users are Gen X and Millennials at 79% and 81% respectively (source).
    • Net Promoter Scores for merchants are 8 points higher when using PayPal (source).

    For more information on how you can leverage this new partnership to enhance your business, speak to one of our experts.

    **Note: Available to US customers only for the time being.*
