    IAAPA 2025: A Week on the Floor - and a Night to Remember

    By Geraldine Lee · Nov 26, 2025 · Updated Mar 13, 2026 · RocketRez News

    On this page
    Showing Up Strong on the Show FloorThe Nightcap 2025: Where the Real Conversations HappenedSee You Next Year

    IAAPA brings the entire attractions world together, but this year had a different energy for us. From packed demos at Booth #4459 to conversations that stretched long after show hours, the week was full of momentum, insight, and genuinely great people.****

    Showing Up Strong on the Show Floor

    Our team rolled in representing RocketRez head-to-toe, literally, in our RocketRez jerseys.

    Across the week, we ran back-to-back demos, met operators from every corner of the industry, and had some of our most productive conversations of the year. IAAPA was busy, but in the good way, the kind of busy where real partnerships form.

    The Nightcap 2025: Where the Real Conversations Happened

    On Wednesday evening, we were fortunate enough to host The Nightcap, an exclusive happy hour curated for operators, partners, and friends of the industry.

    But an evening like this doesn't come together alone. We were lucky to have sponsors who shared the same vision: no pressure, no sales pitches, just good company and real conversation.

    Together, we kept the music low enough for people to actually talk, brought in a professional cigar roller for those who wanted to unwind in style, and made sure the drinks and food were flowing.

    The goal was simple: give IAAPA attendees exactly what they needed after a long day on the floor.

    The feedback was incredible. Guests appreciated the relaxed atmosphere where they could network authentically and enjoy themselves without feeling like they were at another vendor event. That was exactly the point.

    Thank You to Our Sponsors

    None of this would have been possible without the support of our sponsors who helped bring The Nightcap to life. Your partnership made it possible to create an evening that people are still talking about.

    See You Next Year

    IAAPA 2025 reminded us why we love this industry. The passion, creativity, and commitment to creating amazing guest experiences is evident in everyone we meet.

    We're already planning for next year's Nightcap, and we want you there. Pre-register your interest below to be the first to know when invitations go out for IAAPA 2026.

    Thank you to everyone who stopped by our booth or joined us at The Nightcap. Here's to creating more unforgettable experiences together.

    GL
