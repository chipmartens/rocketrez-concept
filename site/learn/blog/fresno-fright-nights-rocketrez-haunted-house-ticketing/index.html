    Fresno Fright Nights Selects RocketRez to Power Ticketing and Guest Experience at Premier Haunted House Attraction

    By Matt Lederman · Oct 2, 2025 · Press Release

    On this page
    A Frighteningly Good Guest ExperienceFast Track to Launch

    RocketRez, the enterprise platform for attractions and seasonal experiences, is proud to announce a new partnership with Fresno Fright Nights, a large-scale haunted house attraction launching in Fresno, California.

    RocketRez will provide the technology backbone for FFN’s ticketing, on-site point of sale, and food & beverage operations in time for the 2025 haunt season.

    A Frighteningly Good Guest Experience

    Fresno Fright Nights is bringing a new level of immersive horror entertainment to the Central Valley. Designed as a high-capacity seasonal attraction, FFN offers multiple ticket tiers, live entertainment, F&B options, and a growing fan base of Halloween thrill-seekers.

    To support its ambitious plans, FFN has selected RocketRez to launch a fully integrated commerce and operations platform featuring:

    • Timed and tiered ticketing options (GA, VIP, and bundled packages) ****
    • Food sales support using RocketRez’s F&B module ****
    • Mobile-friendly online checkout to drive pre-season revenue ****
    • In-person POS for ticketing, F&B, and basic retail sales ****
    • Email and SMS marketing automation to engage guests and drive repeat visits

    “Launching a brand-new haunted house is about more than just scary sets, it’s about delivering a premium guest experience at every touchpoint,” said Bob Martin, Managing Partner of Fresno Fright Nights. “RocketRez gives us the flexibility, speed, and tools we need to execute at scale.”

    Fast Track to Launch

    RocketRez will also support the configuration of tiered product bundles and lean F&B/retail menus-all with real-time reporting to power smarter decisions throughout the season.

    “Fresno Fright Nights is launching with big ambitions, and we’re honored to be their technology partner,” said Sean Harvey, Chief Revenue Officer at RocketRez. “This is a haunted house built for high-volume demand, and RocketRez is built to handle it.”

    For more information about RocketRez and our tailored solutions for haunted attractions, seasonal events, and ticketed experiences, book a consultation with our experts.
