    Introducing RocketRez Insights: A Flexible Suite of Reporting Tools to Access Your Data, Your Way

    By Geraldine Lee · Sep 10, 2024 · Updated Sep 24, 2024 · RocketRez News

    On this page
    Understanding RocketRez InsightsKey Features of RocketRez InsightsDesigned with You in MindEmpowering Data-Driven DecisionsTry it Yourself

    Today, we're excited to announce the launch of RocketRez Insights, our new comprehensive reporting suite designed specifically for the tours and attractions industry. This powerful tool is now available to help operators gain deeper understanding and control over their business data.

    Understanding RocketRez Insights

    RocketRez Insights is our response to the evolving needs of tour and attraction operators. We've created a flexible, robust reporting tool that adapts to various business needs, whether you're running a single location or managing multiple attractions.

    Key Features of RocketRez Insights

    1. Prebuilt Dashboards and Reports: Access pre-configured reports and live dashboards, offering quick insights into various aspects of your business.
    2. Power BI Integration: For more advanced users, our Power BI integration allows for custom dashboard creation and in-depth data analysis.
    3. Excel Export Capability: Easily export your data to Excel for further customization and analysis.
    4. API Access: Seamlessly connect your data with third-party software through our API.

    Designed with You in Mind

    We developed RocketRez Insights based on extensive feedback from industry professionals. As John Pendergrast, our CEO, explains: "This launch is about giving our customers the flexibility to use their data in ways that make the most sense for their business."

    Empowering Data-Driven Decisions

    Whether you're looking to understand daily sales trends, analyze visitor demographics, or dive deep into operational metrics, RocketRez Insights provides the tools you need to make informed decisions.

    Try it Yourself

    Ready to see RocketRez Insights in action? Experience the power of our new reporting suite firsthand with our interactive demo.
