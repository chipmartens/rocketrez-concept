    Boost Your Business Operations with RocketRez's Tested Hardware Recommendations

    By Geraldine Lee · Jul 21, 2023 · Updated May 28, 2025 · Business Operations

    On this page

    Imagine running your tour or attraction with seamless efficiency, high-tech hardware, and personalized support at every turn. With RocketRez, this is not just a dream, it's reality.

    In the fast-paced, customer-centric world, having the right tools to streamline your operations is crucial.

    This is where RocketRez comes in, offering unmatched flexibility and thoroughly tested hardware recommendations tailored to your unique business needs.

    RocketRez's Flexibility

    At the heart of RocketRez's appeal is our remarkable flexibility. Our software is compatible with any device that runs a 64-bit Windows operating system.

    Whether you operate a sightseeing boat tour or manage a bustling wildlife park, you can seamlessly integrate RocketRez into your current hardware setup, enhancing efficiency without disrupting your existing operations.

    RocketRez's Expertise and Advice

    Every tour or attraction has unique operational needs, and our team is dedicated to understanding yours. We provide personalized advice, ensuring you choose the best hardware for your specific circumstances.

    Whether you need scanners for high-speed ticketing or robust POS systems for your gift shop, we've got you covered.

    Tested and Approved Hardware

    Not all hardware options are created equal, which is why we take a personalized approach when discussing hardware needs with our customers.

    We understand that every business operates differently, and that's why we have conversations with our clients to understand their specific requirements and existing hardware.

    Based on these discussions, we provide recommendations tailored to their unique business needs. This ensures seamless compatibility with RocketRez and allows them to make informed decisions about their hardware components.

    To offer an even more comprehensive understanding of our hardware recommendations, we've broken them down into a digestible list. Each category contains items designed to meet specific operational needs within your tours or attractions business.

    • Points of Sale: This is the star of your show. Our recommended POS systems are designed to be fast, reliable, and user-friendly to make your checkout process quick and efficient.
    • Scanners: Whether you're scanning tickets or barcodes, you need a reliable scanner that can process hundreds of items in seconds.
    • Cash Drawers: Keep your money safe with one of our recommended cash drawers. They come in a variety of sizes to fit every business's needs, whether you’re based in the US or Canada.
    • Ticket Printers: Printing tickets quickly and efficiently is essential to any successful attraction. You can trust our recommendations to get the job done every time.
    • Receipt Printers: Reliable printers for clear, quick receipt printing.
    • Label Printers: Ideal for creating durable labels for a variety of uses.
    • ID Card Printers: Perfect for creating professional quality membership cards on-site.
    • Passport Readers: Streamline your check-in process with easy-to-use passport readers.
    • Mobile Sales: Includes tablets (WiFi) and rugged cases for secure mobile transactions.
    • Mobile Receipts: Portable solutions for instant receipt issuance.
    • Kiosk Display Computers: Self-service options for improved customer experience.
    • Kiosks Stands: Includes countertop stands and floor stands for versatile kiosk placement.
    • Kiosk Add-Ons: Enhance your kiosk's functionality with add-ons like ticket scanners, receipt/ticket printers, and EMV cradles like VersaPay Wallaby, Shift4 Wallaby PRO, Shift4 Wallaby, and CardKnox Wallaby.

    Each of these categories includes hardware that has been thoroughly tested and approved by our team for compatibility with RocketRez.

    Customized Hardware Recommendations

    At RocketRez, we acknowledge that every business is unique. That's why we offer customized hardware recommendations to enhance your operations.

    Perhaps your aquarium could benefit from a self-service kiosk to speed up ticket purchases, or your transportation ferry could use a mobile sales tablet for on-the-go transactions.

    Whatever your needs, our team is here to guide you toward the most beneficial hardware for your operations.
