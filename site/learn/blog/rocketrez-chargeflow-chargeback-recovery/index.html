    How Tour & Attraction Operators Are Recovering Lost Revenue with RocketRez + Chargeflow

    By Matt Lederman · Feb 5, 2026 · Integrations

    On this page
    The ProblemThe SolutionWhy This Matters for Tours & AttractionsReady to Start Winning Your Chargebacks?

    Chargebacks and fraud are a reality for any business that accepts credit cards, but for tours and attractions, the impact can be especially painful.

    High-ticket purchases, advance bookings, and peak-season sales mean every lost dispute is money that could have been reinvested into your guest experience.

    The Problem

    Many operators view chargebacks as an unavoidable expense. Without the time or expertise to build strong cases, disputes go uncontested, and revenue is lost. In fact, up to 99% of winnable chargebacks in our industry are never fought.

    The Solution

    RocketRez has partnered with Chargeflow, the AI-driven chargeback solution trusted by over 15,000 merchants worldwide. Chargeflow automates the entire dispute process, from gathering evidence to submitting responses, using real-time data from your RocketRez platform.

    Why This Matters for Tours & Attractions

    • Hands-off recovery: No need to log into third-party software or manage dispute workflows.
    • Tailored for travel disputes: Chargeflow's AI builds industry-specific evidence packages to maximize your win rate.
    • Proven results: Operators in our sector see an average 60% win rate, recovering thousands of dollars each year.
    • Risk-free pricing: Pay Chargeflow only when you win.

    Ready to Start Winning Your Chargebacks?

    Chargebacks don't have to drain your revenue. With RocketRez and Chargeflow, disputes turn into recovered dollars, without lifting a finger.

    Already a RocketRez customer?

    Reach out to your Customer Success Manager to get Chargeflow enabled on your account. Setup takes just minutes, and you'll be fighting chargebacks automatically in no time.
