    RocketRez partners with Shift4 Payments

    By Matt Lederman · Jun 22, 2021 · Updated Feb 3, 2026 · RocketRez News

    On this page
    Secure Payment Processing Solutions for Tour and Attraction…

    RocketRez customers gain access to industry leading payment processing technology and exclusive offers.

    Tour and Attraction businesses accept payments in many ways. Online, point-of-sale, kiosk and mobile transactions must be seamlessly cleared with a central data source to report revenue and gain customer insights.

    RocketRez is excited to announce our integration with payment processor Shift4 Payments, which comes with an exclusive offer for our customer base.

    Secure Payment Processing Solutions for Tour and Attraction Businesses

    Shift4 will integrate with RocketRez tools, reflecting your brand and fitting into your pre-determined sales process. At over 200 billion dollars processed annually, Shift4 users can be confident they are joining a large and trusted platform with several scale advantages:

    • Security and Compliance: Secure payment gateway with EMV, PCI-validated P2PE, and tokenization.
    • Hardware: Free P2PE-enabled EMV terminals with free lifetime replacements.
    • Reporting: Advanced accounting, auditing, and reporting capabilities.

    At a time when many Tour and Attraction operators are still under social distancing mandates in the wake of COVID-19, Shift4 Payments is an industry leader in contactless payment technology.
