    RocketRez Announces Integration with Capturelife to Help Attractions Turn Guest Memories into Revenue

    By Matt Lederman · Jul 22, 2025 · Updated Aug 22, 2025 · Press Release

    On this page
    A Smarter Way to Deliver and Sell Guest PhotosA Perfect Fit for RocketRez Customers

    RocketRez, the leading ticketing and operations platform for tours and attractions, is excited to announce a new integration with Capturelife, a cutting-edge digital souvenir platform that enables attractions to deliver and monetize guest photos by fully integrating them with their ticketing and point of sale system.

    This partnership brings together two industry leaders committed to helping attractions deliver exceptional guest experiences while unlocking new revenue opportunities.

    A Smarter Way to Deliver and Sell Guest Photos

    Capturelife is a modern, digital-first platform that replaces outdated photo sales booths and cumbersome manual processes with a seamless, automated workflow. Attractions can now deliver branded souvenir photos instantly to guests’ smartphones by associating them with their ticket as soon as they go in, without requiring app downloads or manual entry.

    Guests have the option to pre-purchase those photo packages as part of their ticket booking process. However, if they choose not to, guests can still view, share, and purchase high-quality digital or printed photos from their mobile device or on-site through a POS or kiosk. Capturelife’s fully branded storefront supports multiple languages and currencies, enabling attractions to sell globally with full GDPR compliance.

    A Perfect Fit for RocketRez Customers

    Attractions using RocketRez are already looking for ways to unify the guest journey-before, during, and after a visit. By integrating Capturelife directly into the RocketRez ecosystem, operators can now:

    • Enhance the in-venue experience with instant, touchless photo delivery
    • Increase per-capita spending with effortless upsell opportunities
    • Extend the guest relationship beyond the visit with automated post-trip follow-ups
    • Unlock a new revenue stream with little to no additional staff or infrastructure

    Attractions currently using Capturelife report 15-25% more revenue after guests leave, thanks to automated post-visit remarketing and compelling photo products.

    “RocketRez is all about helping attractions modernize their operations and grow revenue across the entire guest journey,” said Sean Harvey, Chief Revenue Officer at RocketRez.

    “Photos are an essential aspect of the guest experience. What we’ve found, however, is that a lot of attractions offer photo souvenirs, but it’s a revenue stream that is completely siloed from their main ticketing platform. By integrating with Capturelife, we’re giving our customers an elegant, low-effort way to offer beautiful souvenirs that guests actually want, and buy-long after they’ve gone home.”
    To learn more about the RocketRez + Capturelife integration, schedule a personalized demo here.
