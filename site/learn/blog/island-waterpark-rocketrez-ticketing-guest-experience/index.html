    Island Waterpark Selects RocketRez to Power Modern Guest Experience and Streamline Operations

    By Matt Lederman · Sep 10, 2025 · Press Release

    On this page
    A Modern Platform for a Seasonal PowerhousePhased RolloutBuilt for Operators. Loved by Guests.

    RocketRez, the enterprise platform for high-performing attractions, is proud to announce a new partnership with Island Waterpark, a premier family waterpark located in Fresno, California.

    Related: RocketRez for waterparks

    Known for its thrilling water rides, private cabanas, and large-scale group events, Island Waterpark will roll out RocketRez’s all-in-one platform.

    A Modern Platform for a Seasonal Powerhouse

    Island Waterpark attracts thousands of guests each season with its immersive attractions and welcoming atmosphere.

    To better serve guests and streamline operations, the leadership team selected RocketRez to replace legacy systems with a single, modern platform purpose-built for seasonal attractions.

    “We were ready to move on from disconnected systems and provide a guest experience that matches the quality of the park,” said Matt Massey for Island Waterpark. “RocketRez brings everything together, from online sales to food ordering to group events, and makes it feel effortless.”

    Phased Rollout

    The implementation of RocketRez includes full deployment of:

    • Season Passes & Digital Passes
    • Mobile-friendly checkout
    • Day ticketing
    • Cabana bookings
    • Food & beverage operations
    • Group and private event rentals
    • Retail and upsells
    • Guest communications via email and SMS

    Built for Operators. Loved by Guests.

    By transitioning to RocketRez, Island Waterpark will consolidate tools previously spread across multiple vendors into one unified platform-eliminating manual reconciliation and improving real-time visibility into sales, redemptions, and guest behavior.

    “Island Waterpark is a cornerstone of summer fun in California’s Central Valley,” said Sean Harvey, Chief Revenue Officer at RocketRez.

    “They needed a platform that could handle the complexity of seasonal demand without creating friction for staff or guests. We’re proud to help them deliver their best season yet.”
