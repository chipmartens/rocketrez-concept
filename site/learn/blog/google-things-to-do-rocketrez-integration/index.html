    Leverage Google Things to Do with RocketRez and Unlock More Tour Bookings

    By Geraldine Lee · Jun 21, 2023 · Updated Feb 3, 2026 · RocketRez News

    On this page
    What is Google Things To Do?How Does RocketRez's Integration with Google Things To Do…Benefits of Using Google Things To Do

    Imagine increasing your tour bookings and attracting more customers with just a few simple clicks.

    By harnessing the power of Google Things to Do and RocketRez's seamless integration, you can elevate your business to new heights.

    Dive in to discover how this game-changing integration can help you unlock more tour bookings and grow your business.

    What is Google Things To Do?

    Google Things to Do (GTTD) is a travel planning tool that helps users discover and book tours, attractions, and activities with ease. Seamlessly integrated with Google Search and Google Maps, it enables users to easily find things to do when they are planning a trip.

    An example of an attraction listed on the Google Things to Do platform.

    When you connect your Google Business Profile to GTTD, you can list your tours and make them discoverable when someone is searching for activities or attractions in your area.

    It's perfect for tapping into a vast pool of potential customers who are actively searching for things to do in your area.

    The best part?

    RocketRez offers this integration at no additional cost, whereas other res-tech providers may charge extra fees for similar services.

    How Does RocketRez's Integration with Google Things To Do Work?

    RocketRez is an official connectivity partner of Google, so you can trust that our integration will help you get maximum results out of your GTTD profile.

    Here's how you can get started:

    Step 1: Claim Your Google Business Profile

    Before you can integrate Google Things to Do with RocketRez, you should claim your Google Business Profile.

    While it’s not actually a requirement for you to show up on GTTD, it’s important for any business that wants to be found on Google Search and Maps.

    Visit the Google My Business website and follow the instructions to verify your business.

    Step 2: Optimize Your Profile for Bookings

    After claiming your business profile, optimize it by adding high-quality photos, accurate business information, and engaging descriptions of your tours and attractions. This will make your offerings more appealing to potential customers.

    If you need help with crafting appealing copy, check out our guide on how to use ChatGPT to supercharge your marketing content .

    Step 3: Set up Featured Tours for Google Things To Do

    To enable Google Things to Do in RocketRez, follow these steps:

    1. Go to Menu > Settings > Company > Tours > Location. Select the tour you wish to enable Google Things To Do for, and click on "+ Add" below Google Places Location. Choose a Location Type from the first three options: Admission Ticket, Related no Admission, or Supplementary Add on. Then, search for your location by entering your office or tour place address.
    2. In the Tours > Info tab, fill in the description and images of the tour, as this is a required setup. Follow this path: Menu > Settings > Company > Tours > Info > Enter Tour Description and upload images.
    3. Go to Menu > Settings > Marketing > Web Sales. Select the web engine you wish to enable for Google Things To Do, then go to Config > Enable Google Things To Do > Options. Enter the URL of the booking page where your web engine is hosted.

    Step 4: Manage Bookings and Availability with RocketRez Dashboard

    Once you've set up Google Things to Do, you will now have access to an additional channel to sell your tickets.

    But it doesn't stop there, since all your sales will flow into your web engine, you will be able to manage your bookings and availability with ease in your RocketRez dashboard.

    Benefits of Using Google Things To Do

    By using Google Things to Do with RocketRez, you can unlock more tour bookings and increase your reach exponentially. Here are some of the benefits:

    • Expand your customer base: Tap into a vast audience of potential clients actively searching for tours and attractions on Google.
    • Streamlined booking experience: Provide a seamless and user-friendly booking process for customers, directing them straight from your official listing to your booking page.
    • 100% free: Enjoy the benefits of the RocketRez and Google Things to Do integration without worrying about additional fees or commissions.
    • Perfect for businesses of all sizes: No matter if you manage a large-scale attraction or a small group tour, this integration can help you increase your booking and succeed.

    Integrating Google Things to Do with RocketRez is a game-changing opportunity for tour operators and attraction providers looking to increase their reach and boost their bookings.

    By leveraging this powerful and free integration, you can ensure your business stays ahead of the competition and continues to grow.
