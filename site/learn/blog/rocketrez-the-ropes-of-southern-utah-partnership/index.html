    RocketRez Announces New Partnership with The Ropes of Southern Utah to Enhance Guest Experience and Streamline Operations

    By Matt Lederman · May 31, 2024 · Updated May 28, 2025 · RocketRez News

    On this page
    Integrated Software Solutions for Multi-Attraction…

    RocketRez, a leading provider of software solutions for the tourism and attractions industry, is thrilled to announce a new partnership with The Ropes of Southern Utah, an innovative adventure and entertainment complex located in the heart of Utah’s scenic landscapes.

    This partnership aims to deploy RocketRez’s cutting-edge technology platform to streamline operations and enhance guest experiences across the facility’s diverse attractions.

    “We are excited to partner with RocketRez to leverage their robust software solutions that will allow us to manage our complex operations smoothly and enhance our service offerings. This technology will not only help us streamline our admissions and on-site retail and dining services but also greatly improve our capacity to host and manage events efficiently.”

    John Hendleman

    Owner of The Ropes of Southern Utah

    The Ropes of Southern Utah, set to feature a ropes course, golf simulator rooms, a virtual reality platform, and multiple dining establishments, has faced the challenge of integrating these varied services into a cohesive operational system. The need for efficient management of admissions, retail, food and beverage services, and special events such as birthday parties, weddings, and corporate functions is paramount to ensuring a seamless guest experience.

    Integrated Software Solutions for Multi-Attraction Entertainment Complexes

    To address these challenges, RocketRez will implement an integrated suite of features including advanced admissions systems, retail and food service management, and automated waiver processes. The installation of check-in and indoor kiosks will facilitate quick service and reduce wait times, allowing guests to complete waivers and check into attractions effortlessly.

    “We are excited to partner with RocketRez to leverage their robust software solutions that will allow us to manage our complex operations smoothly and enhance our service offerings,” said John Hendleman, Owner of The Ropes of Southern Utah. “This technology will not only help us streamline our admissions and on-site retail and dining services but also greatly improve our capacity to host and manage events efficiently.”

    “Our collaboration with The Ropes of Southern Utah is an excellent example of how our platform can be customized to meet the unique needs of a multifaceted entertainment complex. By integrating various operational aspects into one platform, we are confident that The Ropes of Southern Utah will deliver an exceptional visitor experience and operate more efficiently.”

    Sean Harvey

    Chief Revenue Officer at RocketRez

    Sean Harvey, Chief Revenue Officer at RocketRez, commented, “Our collaboration with The Ropes of Southern Utah is an excellent example of how our platform can be customized to meet the unique needs of a multifaceted entertainment complex. By integrating various operational aspects into one platform, we are confident that The Ropes of Southern Utah will deliver an exceptional visitor experience and operate more efficiently.”

    The deployment of the RocketRez platform at The Ropes of Southern Utah is designed to enhance operational efficiency, increase revenue through better management of activities and events, and provide guests with a memorable and seamless experience.
