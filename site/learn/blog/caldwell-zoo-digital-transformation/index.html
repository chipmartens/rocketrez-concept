    Digital Innovation Transforms the Caldwell Zoo Experience

    By Geraldine Lee · Jan 20, 2025 · Updated Mar 13, 2026 · RocketRez News

    On this page
    A Natural Evolution in Guest ExperienceWhere Technology Meets NatureBuilding the Future of Wildlife EducationThe Future of Zoo ExperiencesReady to Transform Your Attraction?

    Imagine a zoo where your phone becomes your passport to adventure, where buying tickets, joining as a member, and every visit is an effortless adventure.

    Related: RocketRez for zoos and wildlife parks

    This is the future taking shape at Caldwell Zoo, where a new partnership with RocketRez is transforming the traditional zoo experience from entrance to exit.

    A Natural Evolution in Guest Experience

    Caldwell Zoo isn't just any wildlife sanctuary, it's a beloved institution where conservation meets education, and generations of families come to connect with nature.

    By partnering with RocketRez through a competitive RFP process, Caldwell Zoo has taken a decisive step toward modernizing their entire operational ecosystem.

    "Partnering with RocketRez marks a significant milestone in our journey towards complete digital transformation. Their comprehensive platform will not only streamline our internal processes but will also enhance our ability to deliver exceptional experiences to our visitors."

    Dwight Evans

    Chief Financial Officer at Caldwell Zoo

    Where Technology Meets Nature

    This isn’t just about upgrading ticketing systems, it’s a full-scale reimagining of the visitor experience. RocketRez’s integrated platform will enhance every aspect of the journey:

    • All Day and Timed Ticketing: Flexible options for visitors to plan their perfect day at the zoo.
    • Advanced Point of Sale Systems: For retail and food & beverage operations to enhance transaction efficiency.
    • Membership Management: Streamlined handling of zoo memberships with enhanced customer engagement tools.
    • Forms and Waivers: Digital integration to manage necessary documentation efficiently.
    • Events Management: A robust module to effortlessly schedule and manage zoo events.

    Building the Future of Wildlife Education

    Beyond improving guest experiences, this partnership also addresses critical operational challenges like outdated hardware and server maintenance.

    RocketRez’s cloud-based solutions empower Caldwell Zoo’s staff to focus on what matters most: conservation, education, and creating meaningful connections between humans and wildlife.

    "Our collaboration with Caldwell Zoo is designed to revolutionize their operational capabilities. With our integrated solutions, the zoo will not only see an improvement in operational efficiency but also in how they engage with their guests and manage sensitive data."

    Sean Harvey

    Chief Revenue Officer at RocketRez

    The Future of Zoo Experiences

    With this digital transformation, Caldwell Zoo is raising the bar for modern zoo operations. Guests can look forward to seamless interactions, while the zoo builds a strong foundation for future growth in wildlife education and conservation.
