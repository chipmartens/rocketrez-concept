    November 2020 - Product Updates, New Integrations, and Features!

    By Matt Lederman · Nov 11, 2020 · Updated Feb 3, 2026 · RocketRez News

    On this page
    Update: RocketLinkZapier IntegrationAndroid Scanning AppAffirm IntegrationNew Stripe IntegrationShift 4 Integration

    We've been working hard the last few months to bring some key integrations to our all-in-one platform for tours & attractions. This month we have:

    Update: RocketLink

    RocketLink is your customer-facing portal. It's fully customizable to your brand, and customers can quickly access their account with one link to:

    • Purchase additional tours, tickets, photos, memberships, and so much more.
    • Abandoned Cart Emails and SMS are directed here to complete purchases.
    • RocketPhoto images are accessible to customers here.
    • Forms/Waivers and missing OTA reservation information.
    • Much more!

    Zapier Integration

    RocketRez is all about Automation. We have 100's of Automations within our All-in-One operating system. However, now with our integration to Zapier, you can automate processes with select tools outside of RocketRez.

    We have started with the most popular requests to some marketing platforms like Active Campaign. Lots more "Zaps" planned!

    Android Scanning App

    For our high volume customers or those with operations in various locations like Hop on/off bus tours, validating a ticket is important. The ability to scan a ticket on a mobile device gives the flexibility to check-in/validate customers or scan their multi-use pass.

    We've always offered the RocketScan iOS app. Now we've added all android devices to support a wider range of hardware. Happy scanning.

    Affirm Integration

    Offer a new payment option to your visitors with Affirm, directly integrated into the RocketRez web engine builder.

    Affirm offers your guests the ability to pay for their cart in installments.

    New Stripe Integration

    Stripe gives RocketRez the ability to be a payment Facilitator. This helps us go faster when working with payment processing and as an added benefit, comes with many modern benefits like ACH partner payments, advanced reporting and more.

    Shift 4 Integration

    Shift4 is one of the world's most advanced payment gateways for hospitality businesses, delivering a complete omnichannel ecosystem that extends beyond payments to include a wide range of commerce-enabling services.

    The company’s technologies help power over 350 software providers in numerous industries, including hospitality, retail, F&B, e-commerce, lodging, gaming, and many more. Shift4 has securely processed more than $200 billion in payments volume for over 200,000 businesses in 2019.
