    Strategies to Help Maximize Your Black Friday Sales with PayPal

    On-demand webinar · Oct 15, 2024

    On this page
    About the webinarSpeakersRelated resources

    About the webinar

    Prepare for the busiest shopping season of the year with expert insights from PayPal and RocketRez to help boost your Black Friday sales.

    Join us on Oct 15 as we explore how tours and attractions can leverage innovative payment solutions and strategies to boost sales and drive conversions.

    Did you know?

    • 200.4 million consumers shopped from Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday (NRF)
    • There's a 10% YoY growth in average expected holiday spending on experiences (Deloitte)
    • 66% plan to shop during Thanksgiving week, up from 49% in 2022 (Deloitte)

    In this exclusive webinar, we’ll cover:

    1. Black Friday Best Practices

    Uncover strategies to attract high-value customers who spend nearly 3X more on experiences (Deloitte).

    2. Maximizing Black Friday Opportunities

    Learn actionable tips to refine your marketing strategies and capitalize on the holiday shopping frenzy.

    3. Future-Proof Your Business

    Explore the growing trend of flexible payment options like Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) and how it can help increase cart size by 45% with Pay Later offers.*

    * Based on an internal data analysis of Pay Later retailers, October 2020 through August 2023. Data inclusive of transactions using PayPal Pay Later products across 7 markets (US UK AU DE FR IT ES).

    Speakers

    Emily Schneider

    Emily is the Director of Global Pay Later Success Management at PayPal, specializing in Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) solutions. With over eight years of experience in the payments industry, Emily is dedicated to enhancing merchant growth by providing payment flexibility to their customers.

    Daniel PinoHost

    Daniel Pino is the Director of Business Development at RocketRez, with over 7 years of experience in the travel and hospitality industry. He is known as the Founder and CEO of TourOpp GO!, an automated messaging platform for tour and activity operators, which was acquired by RocketRez.
