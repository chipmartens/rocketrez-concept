    About Paula Panton

    Paula Panton is the founder of Water Safety Products, Inc., a Florida-based international supplier of lifeguard uniforms and safety equipment. After years in the fashion swimwear business, Paula designed her first lifeguard suits for Wet 'n Wild in 1987 and incorporated Water Safety Products in 1989, starting the company out of her home.

    Over nearly four decades, Paula has built the company by listening to operators and turning their ideas into products. Water Safety Products helped introduce the breakaway lanyard, colored rescue tubes, and the Scott Carruthers bead program, and now serves waterparks and aquatic facilities in more than 30 countries. For about ten years, Paula and her team also produced the fashion show that opened the IAAPA Expo.

    About George Panton

    George Panton is Vice President of Water Safety Products, Inc. and President of Satellite Screen Printing, the company's in-house screen printing, embroidery, and transfer printing operation. He began his aquatic career as a Brevard County Ocean Rescue beach lifeguard and is a graduate of the University of Denver.

    After college, George led the pre-opening souvenir photography program at Ferrari World Abu Dhabi and served as the first merchandise manager for Yas Waterworld Abu Dhabi. Since returning to the U.S., he has led customer service, marketing, and product innovation at Water Safety Products, and has visited more than 100 waterparks worldwide.

    Connect with George on LinkedIn.

    About Haley Conrad

    Haley Conrad joined Water Safety Products after a 22-year career at Water Technology, Inc., where she engineered, project managed, and sold waterpark projects across the United States and opened the company's Texas office. A Tulane University engineering graduate, Haley now leads manufacturing and production at Water Safety Products, with a focus on keeping lifeguard swimwear made in the USA.
