    About the show

    Signal cuts through the noise, delivering candid conversations on what truly works (and what doesn't) in today's attraction industry.

    Together, John and Tim combine founder-level vision with hands-on operational expertise.

    Their balanced dialogue, along with insights from expert guests, equips attractions leaders to navigate the future with confidence.

    John PendergrastCo-Host

    As the founder of RocketRez, now its Industry Chairman, John started the company driven by a passion for solving real operational challenges in the industry. For over 23 years, he has worked shoulder-to-shoulder with operators, seeing firsthand the industry’s evolution from gut-feel decisions to data-driven strategy. John offers practical wisdom that connects technological innovation with the real-world challenges of scaling a modern attraction.

    Tim SamsonCo-Host

    As Head of Product at RocketRez, Tim brings decades of operator experience to the conversation. His 27-year career began on the front lines at Cedar Point and evolved into leadership at Morey's Piers, where mentorship from Jack Morey shaped his relentless focus on the guest experience. He provides the on-the-ground realism and strategic foresight that operators need, connecting timeless industry principles with the technology of tomorrow.
