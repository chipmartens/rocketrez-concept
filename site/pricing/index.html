    Before you ask for a quote

    Why isn’t there a price list?

    Two attractions with the same ticket count can run very different operations. A short call on your volume, prices, season and modules gets you a written quote, with every fee in it.

    What does onboarding include?

    A project plan built around your go-live date, data migration for members and products, testing of ticketing, scanning and checkout, staff training, launch-day support and a dedicated customer success manager.

    How long does it take to go live?

    Typical implementation takes 8 to 12 weeks.

    Which hardware works with RocketRez?

    Elo touchscreen terminals, Verifone card readers, Microsoft Surface tablets, Apple iPads, cash drawers and handheld scanners.

    Who processes the payments?

    Stripe, Shift4, PayPal or Sola, with Apple Pay, Google Pay, Affirm, gift cards and charge-to-room. Card processing fees are shown up front.
